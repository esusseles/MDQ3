Projection Settings

Blend weight controls how quickly live results override your pre-election targets. 100% = weight actuals proportionally to % in. Above 100% = trust actuals more aggressively than the linear model (e.g. 150% means at 47% in, you use ~70% actual weight instead of 47%). 0% = always use your pre-election targets.
